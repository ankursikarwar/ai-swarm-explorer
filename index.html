Loading data & interpreting results
1. Prepare on the cluster
The project’s download script saves the source tables in scratch. Its preparation step preserves every JSONL record and creates small files that the explorer can read in batches.
2. Connect through SSH
Run this on your computer and keep the terminal open:
ssh -N -L 8866:127.0.0.1:8866 YOUR_CLUSTER_SSH_ALIAS
Then choose “Connect to cluster.” The page reads through your local tunnel. Large files stay in scratch. Your browser may ask to allow access to your local network.
Alternatively, open a prepared folder
Copy explorer-data to your computer, then select it with “Open data folder.” Only file names are selected initially; records are read when needed. No data is uploaded.
3. Explore and verify
Choose a table, filter records, and open an entry to see its original fields. Search an ID in a related table to follow a session or agent.
Agent statements are claims. Generated summaries may be inaccurate. Review the source’s scaffolding changelog before interpreting changes in behavior.
Screenshots remain in the original daily archives. The record viewer can read a matching image from an archive you select separately.
